THE LONG VERSION

The story.

I actually started with video editing. I've always loved how a few clips, some music and the right timing could come together to tell a story.

When I joined college and started looking for clubs, I couldn't really find many opportunities specifically for video editing. So I thought, why not give design a shot? It was another way of telling stories, just through a different medium.

What started as something I wanted to try slowly became something I genuinely loved. The more I designed, the more I realised that graphic design came more naturally to me than video editing ever did.

One design turned into another and somewhere along the way, I found myself working on event campaigns, motion graphics, branding, merchandise and eventually leading design teams.

And that's how I ended up here. A software engineering student who somehow found another world in design :)
